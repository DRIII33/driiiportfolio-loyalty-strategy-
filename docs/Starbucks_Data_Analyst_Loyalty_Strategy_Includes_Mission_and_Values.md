* **Role Title:** Data Analyst, Loyalty Strategy  
* **Job ID:** 260079240

* **Level:** Individual Contributor  
* **Compensation Range:** \$109,300 – \$178,300 Annually (Bonus Eligible)

## **Role Summary & Strategic Impact**

This role contributes to organizational success by leveraging Starbucks Rewards—an industry-leading loyalty program and key driver of corporate growth. Operating within the Loyalty Strategy & Performance team at the intersection of digital experiences and general management, this position helps optimize loyalty program strategy and define future customer engagement initiatives.

The Loyalty Strategy Data Analyst focuses on key strategic objectives, working cross-functionally across business owners, marketing, technology, and other internal partners to deliver business impact and customer experiences.

## **Key Responsibilities**

### **Strategic Analytics & Customer Insights**

* Explore owned, syndicated, and behavioral data to uncover customer insights, trends, and growth opportunities that support near- and long-term roadmaps and business goals.  
* Conduct inquiry-driven data discovery, design analytical frameworks, define metrics to test hypotheses, and manage dashboard and data product enhancements.  
* Proactively identify and propose analytics tools and solutions to increase efficiency, speed to insight, and speed to product feature development.

### **Business Performance & Execution**

* Analyze business performance by assessing historical trends and establishing forward-looking KPIs and methodologies to drive lifetime value (LTV) and brand engagement.  
* Lead cross-functional teams through complex and ambiguous challenges by identifying core issues, formulating hypotheses, and designing necessary analytical frameworks.  
    
* Craft data-driven narratives for executive leadership, supporting quarterly business performance reviews, key project evaluations, and strategic inquiries.


## **Qualifications & Candidate Profile**

### **Required Professional Experience**

* **Business Analysis, Data Analysis & Modeling:** 4+ years  
* **Managing & Analyzing Business Performance:** 4+ years  
* **Project Ownership & Cross-Functional Leadership:** 2+ years  
* **Executive-Level Presentations:** 2+ years developing and delivering insights to senior leadership


### **Technical Skills & Tooling**

* Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.  
* Demonstrated proficiency with complex databases (e.g., Tableau) to manipulate data, assemble datasets, and evaluate models.  
* Preferred / Nice-to-Have Skills:

  * Basic understanding of data modeling.  
  * Proficiency in SQL.  
  * Hands-on experience building interactive dashboards.

### **Executive Core Competencies**

* **Analytical & Financial Acumen:** Ability to synthesize complex, multi-source data alongside broader business context to establish logical storylines and actionable recommendations.  
* **Problem Structuring:** Skill in clarifying, structuring, and simplifying ambiguous operational and strategic problems.  
* **Communication & Collaboration:** Written and verbal articulation with a track record of building effective relationships across cross-functional matrix teams.  
* **Curiosity & Thought Leadership:** Persistent drive to contribute to collective team knowledge and innovate existing processes.

### **Education**

* MBA, Decision Science, or other quantitative degree/background preferred.
