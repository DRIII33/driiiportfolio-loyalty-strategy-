# Executive Summary: Seattle Market Loyalty Strategy & Tier Friction Analysis

## Strategic Context
Starbucks Rewards is a cornerstone of digital partner impact and enterprise growth[cite: 6]. This project investigates tier migration friction and Reserve product adoption across Seattle urban formats.

## Key Econometric Findings
- **App Feature Association:** OLS regression with HC3 robust standard errors estimates an adjusted conditional association of **$42.93** in total spend associated with mobile app feature adoption (95% CI: $42.05 to $43.82; $p < 0.001$), controlling for tenure and loyalty tier. This represents an adjusted association rather than an unconfounded causal effect.
- **200-Star Threshold Dynamics:** Local RDD modeling estimates an immediate cutoff jump of **-$1.91** ($p = 0.150$), which is not statistically significant. However, the slope interaction term is **-0.3226** ($p < 0.001$), indicating a statistically significant shift in the spending slope across the threshold.
- **Seattle Retention Impact:** Multi-format Seattle engagement (Drive-Thru combined with urban Reserve Roasteries) significantly suppresses customer churn hazard rates.
