# Causal Inference & Econometric Methodology

## Methodological Rationale
1. **OLS with HC3 Robust Standard Errors:** Corrects for heteroskedasticity in heavy-tailed customer spending distributions across tiers, yielding robust standard errors while maintaining associational rigor.
2. **Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD):** Examines spending behavior around the 200-Star threshold. Distinguishes between immediate cutoff jumps and local slope changes, recognizing that synthetic thresholds validate pipeline implementation rather than empirical consumer behavior without robustness diagnostics.
3. **Cox Proportional Hazards Model:** Evaluates customer churn and tier migration hazard rates over time across right-censored customer tenure data.
